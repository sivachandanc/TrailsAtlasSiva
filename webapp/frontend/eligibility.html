      Step 2: Eligibility criteria
      
            
      An AI pharmacist reads each inclusion and exclusion criterion, looks up real ICD-10-CM codes and drugs, and turns what claims can answer into filters. Everything it looked up is shown below.
      
          
      
        
      

        
      
          
      
            
      
              
      What it does
      
              
        
                
      • Splits the criteria into single rules, and decides for each one whether a member must have it or must not have it.
        • 
                
      • Finds ICD-10-CM codes through UMLS and drugs through RxNorm, then expands drugs to every NDC.
        • 
                
      • Reads time windows such as “in the past 3 months”.
        • 
                
      • Keeps rules that claims can't answer (lab values, consent, language) as “needs review” instead of guessing.
        • 
              
      
            
      
            
      
              
      What it sees
      
              
      This trial's eligibility criteria (the only source of rules), plus its titles, summary, interventions and arms to help read them. It also gets the member tables' column names, so it knows what claims can check, and the results of its own lookups. No member data is sent.
      
              
      Cost
      
              
      Each run calls Claude Opus 5.5 and costs roughly $0.20–0.50. Results are kept for a day, so reopening this step is free.
